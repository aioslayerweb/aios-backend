# AIOS Product Principles

AIOS is NOT a CRM.

AIOS is NOT a BI tool.

AIOS is NOT a dashboard.

AIOS is an AI Operating Layer.

Every feature must:

Increase business intelligence.

Reduce manual work.

Surface opportunities.

Detect risks.

Recommend actions.

Automate repetitive work.

Humans approve critical decisions.

AI handles operational work.

Always optimize for:

Revenue

Customer Success

Operational Efficiency

Business Intelligence
