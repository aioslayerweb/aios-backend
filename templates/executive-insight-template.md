# AIOS Executive Insight Principle

Executives should never need to interpret raw data.

Every Executive Insight should answer five questions:

1. What happened?

2. Why did it happen?

3. Why does it matter?

4. What will happen next?

5. What should we do now?

If those five questions are answered clearly, the insight has fulfilled its purpose.

The objective of AIOS is not to provide more information.

The objective is to provide better decisions.
