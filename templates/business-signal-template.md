# AIOS Business Signal Principle

Business Signals are the nervous system of AIOS.

Every important business event should become a signal.

Signals should not merely report events.

They should:

Observe.

Explain.

Prioritize.

Predict.

Recommend.

Trigger automation.

Learn from outcomes.

Every AI Agent should reason using Business Signals rather than isolated data points.

Signals are the language through which AIOS understands a business.
