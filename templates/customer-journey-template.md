# AIOS Customer Journey Principle

A customer journey is not a CRM timeline.

It is a living business narrative.

AIOS should continuously understand:

Who the customer is.

What they care about.

What has happened.

Why it happened.

How the relationship is evolving.

What should happen next.

Every interaction should make AIOS understand the customer better than before.

The objective is not to remember conversations.

The objective is to build an intelligent understanding of the relationship that helps humans create stronger, longer-lasting business partnerships.
