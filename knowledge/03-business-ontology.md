# AIOS Business Ontology

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official business vocabulary of AIOS.

Every AI Agent.

Every developer.

Every prompt.

Every API.

Every workflow.

Every UI.

Should use these definitions consistently.

If a concept is defined here, use it.

Do not invent alternative terminology.

---

# Core Philosophy

AIOS does not organize businesses around software modules.

AIOS organizes businesses around business intelligence objects.

These objects describe how AIOS understands reality.

---

# Business Event

Definition

Something that happened.

Examples

Customer signed a contract.

Invoice became overdue.

Meeting finished.

Employee hired.

Marketing campaign launched.

A Business Event is raw reality.

It has no intelligence by itself.

---

# Business Signal

Definition

An interpreted business event that has meaning.

Purpose

Signals are how AIOS observes businesses.

Examples

Revenue slowing.

Customer engagement increasing.

Pipeline risk detected.

Support SLA breached.

Marketing ROI improving.

Signals contain:

Business meaning.

Priority.

Confidence.

Urgency.

Context.

Signals are the nervous system of AIOS.

---

# Memory Object

Definition

Persistent business knowledge created from important experiences.

Purpose

Allows AIOS to remember.

Examples

Customer history.

Executive decisions.

Meeting summaries.

Lessons learned.

Project outcomes.

AI recommendations.

Memories improve future reasoning.

---

# Knowledge Object

Definition

Reusable business knowledge.

Purpose

Teach AI.

Examples

Sales playbooks.

Pricing strategy.

Executive policies.

Hiring framework.

Risk methodology.

Negotiation best practices.

Knowledge Objects are reusable intelligence.

---

# Executive Insight

Definition

An AI-generated explanation of an important business situation.

Purpose

Help executives understand.

Every Executive Insight answers:

What happened?

Why?

Why now?

What happens next?

What should we do?

---

# AI Recommendation

Definition

An evidence-based proposal generated by AI.

Purpose

Support better executive decisions.

Every recommendation includes:

Reasoning.

Evidence.

Confidence.

Expected outcome.

Alternative options.

---

# Executive Decision

Definition

A business decision made by a human executive.

Purpose

Transform intelligence into action.

AI recommends.

Humans decide.

AI learns.

---

# Executive Briefing

Definition

A concise executive summary of the current business.

Purpose

Replace manual reporting.

Should answer:

What matters today?

Why?

What changed?

What needs attention?

---

# Customer Journey

Definition

The continuously evolving understanding of a customer relationship.

Purpose

Understand customers rather than simply record interactions.

Includes:

Timeline.

Memory.

Signals.

Insights.

Recommendations.

Predictions.

Relationship quality.

---

# Autonomous Task

Definition

An intelligent business action performed by AI.

Unlike automation,

an Autonomous Task reasons before acting.

---

# Autonomous Workflow

Definition

Multiple Autonomous Tasks coordinated to achieve a business objective.

Workflow = coordinated intelligence.

---

# AI Agent

Definition

A specialist AI responsible for one business capability.

Examples

Executive Agent

Revenue Agent

Customer Agent

Finance Agent

Marketing Agent

Workflow Agent

Memory Agent

Risk Agent

Insight Agent

Agents collaborate.

Agents never compete.

---

# Business Context

Definition

Everything AI needs to understand before making a recommendation.

Context includes:

History.

Goals.

Relationships.

Signals.

Memory.

Knowledge.

Industry.

KPIs.

Without context,

AI cannot reason.

---

# Business Intelligence

Definition

Understanding the business.

Not reporting.

Not dashboards.

Not analytics.

Understanding.

---

# Executive Intelligence

Definition

Business intelligence prioritized for executive decision making.

Less information.

More understanding.

---

# Confidence

Definition

AI's measured certainty regarding an insight or recommendation.

Confidence should never imply certainty.

Confidence should always explain:

Supporting evidence.

Missing evidence.

Assumptions.

Unknowns.

---

# Business Value

Definition

The measurable improvement created for the business.

Examples

Revenue increase.

Cost reduction.

Time saved.

Risk reduced.

Customer satisfaction.

Strategic advantage.

Every feature should create business value.

---

# Learning

Definition

The process through which AIOS continuously improves.

Learning occurs after:

Recommendations.

Workflows.

Executive decisions.

Customer interactions.

Business outcomes.

Learning updates Memory and Knowledge.

# Final Principle

Every object in AIOS exists to improve the intelligence of the business.

If a new concept does not strengthen understanding,

it should not become part of the ontology.
