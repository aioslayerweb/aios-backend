# What is AIOS?

AIOS (Artificial Intelligence Operating System) is not another SaaS platform.

It is an AI-powered Company Intelligence Layer that sits above every business system and continuously understands business activity.

AIOS connects data, people, communications and AI into one intelligent operating layer.

Unlike CRMs, BI dashboards or automation platforms, AIOS continuously:

• Understands business context

• Detects opportunities

• Detects risks

• Prioritizes work

• Recommends actions

• Coordinates AI agents

• Automates operational work

• Learns continuously

AIOS is designed to become the AI COO for every company.
