# AIOS Brand Guidelines V1

Status: Official
Owner: Chief Brand Officer, Creative Director, Design Systems Lead
Applies To: Product, Frontend, Website, Marketing, Documentation, AI Output Surfaces

---

## 0. Brand Contract Statement

This document is the single source of truth for AIOS brand expression.

All visual, verbal, interaction, and presentation decisions must align with this guide and remain consistent with the AIOS Constitution, Architecture, Business Ontology, Developer Bible, Hard Rules v2, Design System, Component Library, Frontend Blueprint V1, and Master PRD.

Non-negotiable constraints:

- The uploaded AIOS Pilot logo is the only official logo.
- Do not redesign the logo.
- Do not distort or restyle the identity.
- Brand must communicate trust, clarity, and executive-grade quality.
- Brand must avoid hype aesthetics and speculative AI styling.

---

## 1. Logo Analysis and Core Colour Extraction

The AIOS Pilot logo establishes a restrained, premium palette centered on electric-but-professional blue and deep navy on white.

### 1.1 Suggested Extracted Core Colours

Primary Brand Blue:

- Hex: #1C82F2
- Role: Core brand accent, key actions, emphasis, progress, selected states
- Why it exists: This is the most recognisable visual signal from the ring and Pilot wordmark treatment. It gives clarity and energy without becoming neon.

Dark Navy:

- Hex: #02154A
- Role: Primary logotype tone, authoritative text, dark surfaces
- Why it exists: This is the trust anchor in the identity. It conveys enterprise confidence and long-term stability.

Supporting Blue A:

- Hex: #176EEA
- Role: Hover and active transitions for primary blue elements
- Why it exists: Supports interactive depth while preserving brand continuity.

Supporting Blue B:

- Hex: #4DA1FB
- Role: Soft highlights, subtle accents, information chips, chart support
- Why it exists: Extends brand blue into lighter UI expression without oversaturation.

Very Light Blue:

- Hex: #EAF4FF
- Role: Background tint, soft section dividers, gentle callout containers
- Why it exists: Provides an airy premium backdrop for content-first layouts.

Pure White:

- Hex: #FFFFFF
- Role: Primary background and high-legibility canvas
- Why it exists: Keeps interface clean, timeless, and executive-friendly.

## 3. Logo Guidelines

### 3.1 Approved Logo Forms

- Primary: AIOS Pilot full horizontal lockup
- Horizontal: Symbol + AIOS + PILOT (standard)
- Stacked: Symbol above wordmark only if space-constrained and approved
- Monochrome: Dark Navy on light, White on dark

### 3.2 Minimum Size

- Full logo minimum digital width: 120 px
- Symbol minimum digital width: 24 px

### 3.3 Clear Space

Minimum clear space on all sides equals 0.5x symbol diameter.
No text or UI element may enter this area.

### 3.4 Padding and Placement

- Align to major grid lines
- Keep logo in low-noise zones
- Never place over complex imagery without contrast control

### 3.5 Incorrect Usage

Never:

- Stretch
- Rotate
- Distort
- Recolour outside approved variants
- Add glow, bevel, emboss, shadows, outlines
- Place on clashing gradients
- Change spacing between lockup parts

## 5. Typography

Typography must feel premium, highly legible, and enterprise-native.

### 5.1 Recommended Font Stack

Primary UI Sans:

- Satoshi
- Plus Jakarta Sans
- System fallback: Segoe UI, SF Pro Text, Helvetica Neue, Arial

Display Sans:

- Clash Display
- Satoshi
- System fallback as above

Data Mono:

- IBM Plex Mono
- JetBrains Mono
- ui-monospace fallback

### 5.2 Type Roles and Scale

Display:

- Display XL: 64/72, weight 600, tracking -0.02em
- Display L: 52/60, weight 600, tracking -0.015em
- Display M: 40/48, weight 600, tracking -0.01em

Headings:

- H1: 36/44, weight 600
- H2: 30/38, weight 600
- H3: 24/32, weight 600
- H4: 20/28, weight 600
- H5: 18/26, weight 600
- H6: 16/24, weight 600

Body:

- Body L: 18/30, weight 400 or 500
- Body M: 16/26, weight 400 or 500
- Body S: 14/22, weight 400 or 500

Small and captions:

- Small: 13/20, weight 500
- Caption: 12/18, weight 500

Buttons and navigation:

- Button: 14/20, weight 600
- Nav item: 14/20, weight 500

Tables and forms:

- Table header: 12/18, weight 600
- Table body: 14/20, weight 500
- Input text: 14/22, weight 400 or 500
- Input label: 12/18, weight 600

### 5.3 Spacing and Rhythm Rules

- Paragraph spacing: 1x line height minimum
- Heading-to-body spacing: 16 to 24 px
- Tighten tracking only at display sizes
- Never use ultra-condensed styles for product UI

## 6. Spacing System

### 6.1 8-Point Scale

Base spacing scale:

- 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 120

### 6.2 Margins and Padding

- Page horizontal padding desktop: 40 px
- Page horizontal padding tablet: 24 px
- Page horizontal padding mobile: 16 px
- Card padding: 24 px default
- Dense card padding: 16 px

### 6.3 Section Spacing

- Major section gap: 96 px
- Minor section gap: 64 px
- Component group gap: 24 px

### 6.4 Grid and Containers

- Max content width marketing: 1280 px
- Max content width product: 1440 px
- Standard 12-column grid desktop
- 8-column tablet
- 4-column mobile

### 6.5 Whitespace Philosophy

Whitespace is a strategic design tool, not leftover space.

Rules:

- Prioritize breathing room around decision-critical content
- Avoid dense clusters of competing cards
- Maintain visual silence around headings and primary actions

Allowed sparingly for premium contexts only.

## 10. Motion Guidelines

### 10.1 Motion Philosophy

Motion must support comprehension, orientation, and confidence.

### 10.2 Timing

- Core transition: 0.4 to 0.8 s
- Hover: 0.16 to 0.24 s
- Micro-feedback: 0.2 to 0.4 s

### 10.3 Easing

- Primary: ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)
- Entrance: ease-out
- Exit: ease-in

### 10.4 Hover Behaviour

- Small elevation, border emphasis, or subtle tint
- No aggressive scaling

### 10.5 Page Transitions

- Soft crossfade with slight directional movement
- Keep transitions quick in dense workspaces

### 10.6 Microinteractions

- Button press confirmation
- Field focus transitions
- Validation feedback with clear timing

### 10.7 Loading, Success, Empty

- Use skeletons where layout is known
- Success states should be calm and concise
- Empty states should educate and guide

### 10.8 Motion Restraint Rules

- Never animate every element on page load
- Never chain long decorative sequences in product workflows
- Respect reduced-motion user settings

- Minimal luxury and whitespace confidence

## 14. Accessibility Standards

Minimum standard: WCAG AA+, targeting AAA in key reading and decision surfaces.

Requirements:

- Full keyboard operability
- Visible focus states
- Semantic structure and landmarks
- Reduced motion alternatives
- Contrast-safe colour usage
- Screen reader compatibility for custom components

Do not use colour alone to communicate state.
Always pair semantic colour with icon, text, or pattern.

## 15. Brand Voice and Verbal System

### 15.1 Voice Principles

AIOS voice is:

- Intelligent
- Calm
- Direct
- Precise
- Responsible

### 15.2 Writing Standards by Surface

Website copy:

- Outcome-first messaging
- No hype, no inflated claims
- Clear strategic framing

Buttons:

- Action verbs
- Short and specific labels

Headings:

- Bold but factual
- No jargon stacking

Documentation:

- Structured, explicit, implementation-ready
- Reason and decision context included

Error messages:

- State what happened
- State impact
- State next action

AI responses:

- Explain observation and reasoning
- Include confidence context when relevant
- Avoid anthropomorphic language

### 15.3 Tone Examples

Preferred:

- Here is what changed and why it matters.
- Three actions are recommended based on current risk signals.
- This workflow is paused because approval is required.

Avoid:

- Revolutionary AI magic for your business.
- We guarantee perfect predictions.
- Your assistant has decided for you.
