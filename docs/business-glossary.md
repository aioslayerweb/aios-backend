# AIOS Business Glossary

## Purpose

This document defines the official business terminology used throughout AIOS.

Every AI assistant working on AIOS must use these definitions consistently.

Never invent new terminology if an official AIOS term already exists.

---

# AIOS

Artificial Intelligence Operating System.

AIOS is an AI-powered operating layer that sits above all business systems, continuously understanding business activity, detecting opportunities, identifying risks, recommending actions and automating operational work.

AIOS is NOT a CRM.

AIOS is NOT a dashboard.

AIOS is NOT a BI tool.

AIOS is the Company's Intelligence Layer.

---

# Executive Command Center

The primary dashboard of AIOS.

Purpose:

Provide executives with a complete understanding of the current state of the business.

It answers:

• What requires attention?

• What opportunities exist?

• What risks exist?

• What should AI do?

• What should people do?

It should always prioritize decisions over data.

---

# Executive Overview

The flagship module within the Executive Command Center.

It provides a high-level summary of:

Revenue

Pipeline

Customers

AI activity

Business risks

Business opportunities

Recommendations

KPIs

---

# QBI

Question-Based Intelligence.

QBI allows users to ask business questions in natural language.

Examples:

"Why is pipeline slowing?"

"What customers are likely to churn?"

"What happened this week?"

"What opportunities should I focus on?"

QBI combines structured data, communication history and AI reasoning to produce explainable answers.

---

# Customer Journey Playback

A timeline showing every meaningful customer interaction.

Includes:

Emails

Meetings

Calls

CRM updates

Support tickets

AI summaries

Business signals

Opportunities

Risks

The purpose is to understand the customer's full story in seconds.

---

# Customer Intelligence

The AIOS module responsible for understanding every customer.

Includes:

Customer Health

Relationship history

Buying intent

Expansion opportunities

Risks

Communication analysis

Predictions

Recommendations

---

# Customer Health

An AI-generated score representing the overall health of a customer relationship.

Factors include:

Communication sentiment

Product usage

Support history

Revenue

Response times

Relationship strength

Expansion activity

Health categories:

Excellent

Healthy

Monitor

At Risk

Critical

---

# Opportunity

Any event that could positively impact the business.

Examples:

Upsell

Cross-sell

Renewal

Expansion

High buying intent

Warm lead

Referral

New partnership

Every opportunity should contain:

Description

Business impact

Confidence score

Recommended action

Priority

---

# Risk

Any event that could negatively affect the business.

Examples:

Customer churn

Deal decay

Negative sentiment

Missed follow-up

Support escalation

Low engagement

Contract expiration

Every risk should include:

Severity

Probability

Business impact

Suggested mitigation

---

# Business Signal

Any meaningful event detected by AIOS.

Signals can originate from:

Emails

Meetings

Calls

CRM

Support

Marketing

Product usage

Financial systems

Signals may represent:

Intent

Risk

Opportunity

Urgency

Relationship change

Customer sentiment

Business trend

---

# Signal Detection

The continuous process of scanning all connected systems for meaningful business signals.

Signal Detection never sleeps.

It continuously monitors the organization.

---

# AI Recommendation

A suggested business action generated by AIOS.

Every recommendation must include:

Reason

Business impact

Confidence level

Expected outcome

Recommended owner

Recommended deadline

---

# Recommendation Confidence

AIOS expresses confidence rather than certainty.

Confidence categories:

Very High

High

Medium

Low

Unknown

Never present AI conclusions as absolute facts.

---

An autonomous software agent with a specific business responsibility.

Examples:

Executive Agent

Revenue Agent

Pipeline Agent

Customer Success Agent

Memory Agent

Workflow Agent

Support Agent

Insight Agent

Every AI Agent should:

Observe

Understand

Reason

Recommend

Execute when authorized

Learn

Customer history

Successful actions

Failures

Preferences

Patterns

The Memory System continuously improves future decision quality.

The Data Brain connects:

CRM

Email

Support

Finance

ERP

# Intelligence Engine

The reasoning engine of AIOS.

Predict outcomes

Identify risks

Identify opportunities

Generate recommendations

---

# Workflow

A sequence of business actions.

Workflows may be:

Manual

Semi-automated

AI-assisted

Fully autonomous

# AI Action

An action performed by AIOS.

Examples:

Draft email

Reply

Update CRM

Assign task

Notify manager

Escalate issue

# Human Approval

AIOS always allows humans to approve important actions.

High-impact decisions should require approval.

Routine operational work may be automated.

---

Forecasting

Pipeline risk

---

# Business Intelligence

Within AIOS, Business Intelligence means actionable intelligence.

It is NOT reporting.

It always answers:

What happened?

Why did it happen?

What will happen?

What should happen next?

---

# Insight

An AI-generated understanding of the business.

Insights explain patterns rather than simply displaying data.

Every insight should answer:

What changed?

Why?

Business impact?

Recommended action?

---

# Dashboard

A dashboard in AIOS is not a collection of charts.

Every dashboard should:

Summarize

Prioritize

Explain

Recommend

Enable action

---

# Autonomous Business

The long-term vision of AIOS.

An Autonomous Business continuously:

Monitors itself

Learns

Optimizes

Coordinates AI agents

Reduces manual work

Supports human decision makers

Improves over time

AIOS is the intelligence layer that makes autonomous businesses possible.

---

# Golden Rule

When implementing new features:

Do not build screens that only display information.

Every feature should help users:

Understand.

Decide.

Act.

Improve.

This is the defining principle of AIOS.
